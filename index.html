FAQ

Questions

Does POLLO stream music?

No. POLLO plays the music files already on your phone (MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, WMA). It does not stream or download music.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything a player needs is free and there are no ads. Premium is an optional one-time purchase for extra looks and the monthly and yearly recap.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login, no sign-up and no cloud. Your library, playlists and favorites stay on your phone.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 6.0 or higher.

How do I get the Shinobi theme?

Unlock all the regular achievements — Shinobi is the trophy. It is not part of Premium.
